Purpose of the Study

This study investigates whether gaze-aware reading assistance improves reading comprehension and reduces cognitive load compared to traditional reading methods.

What You Will Do

You will be asked to:

  • Read 4 short passages (approximately 300 words each) using different reading modes: gaze-driven, manual scroll, fixed-pace, and no assistance.
  • Sit in front of a Tobii Eye Tracker 5 during the reading tasks.
  • Complete a brief comprehension quiz after each passage.
  • Complete NASA-TLX (workload) and SUS (usability) questionnaires after each passage.

The session will take approximately 45 minutes.

Data Collected

  • Reading speed and time-on-task
  • Eye gaze patterns (fixations, saccades, regressions)
  • Comprehension quiz responses
  • NASA-TLX and SUS survey answers

Data Handling and Confidentiality

Your study data will be de-identified: you will be assigned a random participant ID, and your name will not be stored alongside your gaze or survey data. The digital data collected during the session (reading speed, gaze patterns, quiz and survey responses) is linked only to your participant ID and stored securely on an encrypted server accessible only to the research team.

This signed consent form contains your name and is stored separately from the digital study data. The two will not be linked in any published results or shared datasets. Aggregate and anonymized findings may be published in academic venues.

Voluntary Participation

Participation is completely voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty. You may withdraw your data after the session by contacting the principal investigator.

Study Title: GARB: Gaze-Aware Reading Aid for the Browser — User Study
Principal Investigator: Christian Nyamekye, Dartmouth College
Contact: christian.k.nyamekye.26@dartmouth.edu

Purpose of the Study

This study investigates whether gaze-aware reading assistance improves reading comprehension and reduces cognitive load compared to traditional reading methods.

What You Will Do

You will be asked to:

  • Read 4 short passages (approximately 300 words each) using different reading modes: gaze-driven, manual scroll, fixed-pace, and no assistance.
  • Wear or sit in front of a Tobii Eye Tracker 5 during the reading tasks.
  • Complete a brief comprehension quiz after each passage.
  • Complete NASA-TLX (workload) and SUS (usability) questionnaires after each passage.

The session will take approximately 45 minutes.

Data Collected

  • Reading speed and time-on-task
  • Eye gaze patterns (fixations, saccades, regressions)
  • Comprehension quiz responses
  • NASA-TLX and SUS survey answers

Data Handling

All data will be anonymized — no personally identifiable information will be linked to your responses. Data is stored securely on garb-api.fly.dev and is accessible only to the research team.

Voluntary Participation

Participation is completely voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty. You may decline to answer any question or withdraw your data after the session by contacting the principal investigator.

Questions

If you have questions about this study, please contact christian.k.nyamekye.26@dartmouth.edu.
